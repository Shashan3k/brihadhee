# Brihadhee — Complete SEO Audit

**Report date:** 13 November 2026, India time. **Website:** https://www.brihadhee.com/
**Business:** South Indian vegetarian restaurant, W Block, Anna Nagar East, Chennai; corporate and event catering.
**Brief:** `keyword (1).md` and the user's complete SEO audit request. This report evaluates organic and local search, not AI-answer visibility.

## Executive summary

An urgent availability incident was observed: on 13 November, both the apex and www domain resolved through DNS but HTTPS connections were reset before an HTTP response was obtained. The same failure occurred in a browser. This establishes a failure on the tested network, not a global outage or its root cause. A later check on 13 November at 16:29 UTC returned HTTP 200 for www and HTTP 308 from apex to www. The reset is no longer reproducible. Record and investigate recurrence; do not describe this as a continuing global outage.

The earlier 12 November public crawl successfully fetched all 17 sitemap pages. The site is a static Astro build with readable HTML, distinct topic pages, canonical URLs and a linked menu/service architecture. The evidence does not support a claim that JavaScript rendering or a missing sitemap is the principal ranking problem.

**The premise that Brihadhee is outside Google's first page for every target keyword is Not Verified.** There is no Search Console query export or location-controlled ranking dataset. A responsible diagnosis distinguishes these possibilities: not indexed, indexed but insufficiently relevant, relevant but less prominent than competitors, or visible in one location/device and not another. Each requires a different response.

Confirmed opportunities include inconsistent public business details, weak public proof for catering capabilities, incomplete practical service information and insufficient measurement. Recent code changes have already addressed the previously reported catering-purity wording and rigid FAQ length rules. Those old findings must not be presented as new unresolved source defects. Production deployment of every recent change requires a successful fresh fetch.

**Recommended commercial focus:** small and mid-size catering from 50 guests, corporate catering, then the distinctive build-your-own dosa and Nellai meals propositions. Broad “best veg restaurant Chennai” terms are relevant but should not displace more specific commercial intents. No ranking improvement is guaranteed.

## 30/60/90-day roadmap

**First 30 days:** Resolve availability and document independent tests; establish Search Console/GBP/ranking/lead baselines; reconcile hours/phone/menu; validate current source changes in production; confirm service/visit facts. Owners: developer for availability/measurement, business owner for facts and listings, SEO lead for query mapping. Success: reliable public access, indexing evidence for every priority page, correct public facts, tested lead delivery.

**By day 60:** Improve small-function and corporate pages with approved service details and two real examples; add current original food/service imagery; complete measured performance fixes and mobile checks; maintain topic ownership and internal links. Success: better documented offer, no material factual contradictions, verified performance change where a defect existed, and a growing set of qualified enquiries with known source.

**By day 90:** Compare the same keyword/location/device panel and GSC periods; assess query-to-page performance, non-brand clicks, Maps interactions and confirmed bookings. Develop genuine local/editorial relationships and only the content supported by demand and kitchen capability. Success is measured improvement against baseline, not a promised top-three or first-page deadline. Account for seasonality and avoid attributing all change to one edit.
