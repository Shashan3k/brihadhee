# Citation and PR plan (Chennai)

Prepared 16 November 2026. **Goal:** more accurate, independent mentions of Brihadhee in places AI engines read and cite. On 12 Nov, Google's AI Overview recommended Brihadhee for build-your-own dosa but cited **Instagram**, with an old phone number. For *best pure veg restaurant in Anna Nagar* it used Business Profile cards. ChatGPT omitted Brihadhee. Independent pages with correct facts give engines something better to cite.

**Rules for every item:**
- No paid links, link exchanges or sponsored posts presented as editorial.
- No review swaps, and no free meals in exchange for reviews.
- Any hosted tasting must be disclosed by the writer, per their own and ASCI rules.
- Send only facts from [`/about/#facts`](https://www.brihadhee.com/about/#facts).
- Never claim "best", "No.1", awards or ratings, catering rates, delivery, parking, Jain or no-onion-no-garlic, Hindi, 2,000-guest events, "premium", seasonal or fasting menus, or the Beyond Curry sub-brand, until the owner approves.
- Outlets named below are well known. The owner should check current contacts and submission rules before sending.

**Standard fact sheet to attach (all verified):** Brihadhee, a pure vegetarian South Indian restaurant on 3rd Avenue, W-Block, Anna Nagar East, Chennai 600040, near Anna Nagar Tower Park. Open daily 11 AM to 11 PM, lunch and dinner only. Dining phone +91 73587 14113. Its specialities:
- Build Your Own Dosa: 5 batters, ₹120–₹145, plus podi, filling and roast, cooked to order;
- Nellai Special Sodhi Meals: ₹240, a Tirunelveli banana-leaf lunch;
- Paanai Parotta: ₹270, parotta and salna slow-cooked in a sealed pot;
- filter coffee: ₹45.

Prices are before 5% GST. Catering: pure vegetarian for every order, 50–1,500 guests per event across Chennai, and 1,000+ corporate meals a day. Catering phone +91 94442 24999. Founded by Rakesh and Eashwer. Website https://www.brihadhee.com/

## How to track

For each outreach, log the date, outlet, contact (internal only), what was sent, and the response in a private sheet. Any resulting mention goes into `docs/geo/visibility-log.csv` with its URL and date, so the monthly AI check (`02-measurement-plan.md`) can see whether engines start citing it.
