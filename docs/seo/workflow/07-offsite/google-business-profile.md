## 8. Ethical review-request process

Follows Google's review policy: no review gating, no incentives, no fake or staff reviews.

1. **Ask every guest the same way.** Don't pick only happy guests or screen them with "Did you enjoy it?" first. That's review gating, and it's prohibited.
2. **When to ask:** when the bill is settled, staff say: "If you have a minute, we'd value a Google review. It helps other families find us." Hand over a small card with a QR code to the Google review link from the profile dashboard ("Ask for reviews" → share link).
3. **No incentives of any kind:** no discount, free coffee or dessert, contest entry or loyalty points for a review. The 10% walk-in offer must never be tied to reviewing.
4. **No fake or staff reviews:** owners, staff, family or agencies must not post reviews, and reviews are never bought or exchanged. Never review competitors.
5. **No review stations:** don't hand guests a restaurant tablet to review on site. Guests use their own phones.
6. **Reply to every review within a week,** positive or negative, using facts:
   - correct wrong details politely, for example "We're open every day, 11 AM to 11 PM", or the dining number +91 73587 14113 if a review quotes an old one;
   - thank the reviewer and name the dish they mentioned;
   - for complaints, apologise, give the dining number, and take it offline.
7. **Never** ask for a review to be removed because it's negative. Report only real policy violations, such as spam or off-topic content.
8. **Website:** no rating or review count is shown and no AggregateRating markup is used until there's dated platform evidence (Q36).
