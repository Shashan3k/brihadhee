It accounts for all 514 source rows in [Appendix A of the SEO audit](../../BRIHADHEE_COMPLETE_SEO_AUDIT.md#appendix-a--keyword-by-keyword-inventory) and includes the two informational or conversational questions requested for Phase 3. ## Priorities and evidence

- **P1** is an existing page with a verified business fit and a clear visit, booking, or qualified catering action. Within P1, prioritize small functions and birthdays from 50 guests, corporate and office meals, event catering and its quote path, then the homepage, dosa, and Nellai lunch pages. Preserve the menu as the branded route for prices and dishes.
- **P2** supports discovery, trip planning, or buyer education. Work on it when a content gap affects the next customer decision. **P3** is broad, mixed, or seasonal intent with lower immediate conversion confidence. **Hold** means a claim needs approval or verification. **Exclude** means the wording falls outside the offer or is explicitly restricted.
- **Source** means the page, menu, or business fact was checked in the repository. **Sample** means public results showed a relevant page type or listing. **Inference** means the intent or opportunity is an editorial judgment. Search volume, difficulty, Google and Maps positions, impressions, clicks, CTR, and conversion rates are **NOT VERIFIED**. The 514 rows are source terms and families, some repeated or malformed.
