## 6. Gaps and opportunities for Brihadhee
- On the six queries tested, none of the five competitors owned the first page; the one Brihadhee appearance was a third-party EazyDiner page for "South Indian restaurant Anna Nagar". Own-site visibility for these terms looks open.
- Sangeetha's menu is image-only with no price text. A crawlable HTML menu with dish names and prices (dosa, parotta, meals, filter coffee) would be a real structural advantage; Gowri Krishnaa's own domain appears lapsed.
- "Banana leaf meals Anna Nagar" returned no confirmed Anna Nagar restaurant, and "filter coffee Anna Nagar" returned cafes, not South Indian veg restaurants. If Brihadhee genuinely serves these, dedicated truthful pages could be uncontested.
- Review volume gap is large: Brihadhee shows 5 reviews on EazyDiner versus Geetham ~1,885 and Gowri Krishnaa ~5,265 Google reviews (aggregator figures). Review generation and photo uploads (11 on EazyDiner versus 26 for Geetham) matter more for the local pack than on-page work.
- Brihadhee's EazyDiner entry says veg and non-veg; confirm the client's real positioning and correct third-party listings, since competitors are largely recorded as "vegetarian".

## 7. Method and limits
- Tools: WebSearch (standard and one extended) and WebFetch on 2026-11-05. WebSearch is US-only and returns model-summarised results; location-dependent differences from Chennai Google results are expected, and no Map Pack was visible.
- Could not open Google Maps, so GBP primary and secondary categories were not observed for any competitor; aggregator tags are labelled as such.
- Several fetches returned empty, 403, 404 or timeouts (Swiggy pages, geethamveg.com content, inkl, Wanderlog), so some figures come from search-result summaries rather than the pages themselves; Swiggy/Zomato ratings are delivery-app ratings, not Google.
- Review counts differ between aggregator snapshots (Geetham 1,211 vs 1,885) and are not live.
- No numbers were estimated. Bhavyam and Rusi Rasa could not be matched to Anna Nagar listings; client should confirm exact names and addresses.
- Next step to close gaps: manual Google Maps check from a Chennai IP for each branch (categories, rating, photo count, "Popular dishes"), and a local-pack check for the six queries.
