# SEO + AEO phase progress

**Phase 1 completed:** 13 November 2026 (Asia/Kolkata). **Scope:** audit reconciliation and plan only. No website source, deployment, or external profile was changed in this phase.

## Decision baseline

The commercial goal is qualified restaurant visits and catering enquiries, with accurate answers wherever customers discover Brihadhee. The next phases should be judged by indexed priority pages, query/page evidence, correct local facts, delivered qualified enquiries, and observed answer accuracy. Checklist scores and keyword counts are diagnostic inputs, not business outcomes.

The evidence order used here is: current source and fresh local build for **implementation**; dated public fetches for **what was live at that time**; owner-confirmed records for **business facts**; and Search Console, Business Profile, enquiry records, and repeatable answer tests for **outcomes**. Older strategy or fixture text does not override newer owner decisions. In particular, `keyword (1).md` (12 November) and `docs/seo/client-confirmations.md` sections F–J supersede older breakfast, tiffin, cheap-meal, 500-dosa and phone wording in `Key.md`, `SEO_RANKING_STRATEGY.md`, and early sections of some operational documents.

**Status key:** `ALREADY_FIXED` = present and passing in current local source/build, with live deployment separately unverified; `CONFIRMED_OPEN` = a reproducible gap in current evidence or an observed public inconsistency; `NOT_VERIFIED` = the needed observation or account data is unavailable; `NEEDS_OWNER_APPROVAL` = a claim, policy, asset or disclosure needs written business approval; `EXTERNAL_ACTION` = a profile/platform change outside this repository. A task may require more than one kind of work; the ledger states its primary status and dependencies.

### Architecture and page ownership
