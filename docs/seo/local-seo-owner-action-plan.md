## Build local evidence customers can use

- **Reviews.** Give all actual diners the same optional request on a receipt or counter QR using the verified profile review link. Invite an honest account in their own words. Use the same approach for real catering customers after the completed service, with separate feedback handling when it is a private event. Avoid rewards, filtering by satisfaction, or requests for prescribed words or ratings. A neutral invitation is “If you’d like to share how your visit went, you can leave a review here.” [Google review guidance](https://support.google.com/business/answer/3474122)
- **Original restaurant photography.** Prioritize recognizable exterior signage, the frontage from both approaches, an honest view of the entrance and dining room, and actual dishes with menu-confirmed captions. Label the same verified photo as illustrative on a catering page if it shows a restaurant dish. The checked interior asset is a placeholder. The repo food images and meal-box files alone cannot authenticate a served event. Capture photo date, file ownership and permission for identifiable people before publication.
- **Approved event examples.** Start with one completed private event and one recurring office programme. Collect date or period, actual guest/meal count, selected dishes, scope of service, original photographs and written permission for any client identity or identifiable guests. A private client may approve an anonymized example. Publish facts only after sign-off.
- **Partnerships.** Discuss a real event-service collaboration with an Anna Nagar or nearby Chennai venue or planner. Ask for a client-facing menu or recap only when it helps their guests and reflects a completed project. For recurring office catering, talk to an office or coworking manager in a confirmed service area about a trial and request an approved factual case only after delivery. These are opportunities to evaluate, with no existing partnership implied.
- **Editorial.** Pitch a kitchen-led Nellai sodhi story and a demonstration of how diners customize a dosa to a Chennai food editor or Tamil food writer. A venue-focused pitch could describe navigating from Anna Nagar East Metro and photographing the real storefront. A title such as Madras Musings is relevant only for a genuine regional food culture angle. Give editors primary materials and independent editorial control. Disclose hosted or paid arrangements and have paid links qualified as sponsored or nofollow. Avoid backlink packages or required reciprocal links.
