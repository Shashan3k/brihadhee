## Approach notes

- Offer an invitation to try the food, not scripted praise or rating requests. If a meal is hosted, the creator should disclose it; do not tie anything to the rating they give.
- Lead with the verifiable facts (address New W-41 (Old W-124), 3rd Avenue, W Block, Anna Nagar East, near Anna Tower; WhatsApp +91 73587 14113) and only claim "vegetarian" after the EazyDiner "veg and non veg" entry is corrected, since creators and AI answers will find it.
- Priority order from the evidence: Zameer bytes (already covered it), Madras Food Kadhaigal and Ekta Kothari (active, vegetarian, Chennai), DT Next (covers new Anna Nagar openings), Akila Kannan Vlogs.

## Method and limits

- Tools: a web search tool (summarised results, possibly US-based, so Chennai and Tamil-language coverage is likely under-represented) plus page fetches and the in-app browser for read-only page views. No sign-ins, accounts, messages or posts.
- Creator counts (followers/subscribers) are only those visible on the profile or channel page on 2026-11-05 and are not audited. Third-party ranking lists (Modash, Heepsy, grynow, findcollab) appeared in search but were not opened, so none are cited.
- Instagram pages were read through a fetch tool that returns limited text; post captions were mostly unavailable, so "what they cover" rests on bio, highlights and visible post descriptions.
- YouTube channel stats were read from the public channel pages; videos were not watched, so nothing here says what those creators said about the food beyond their titles.
- Crazy Masala Food and several others blocked or timed out in the fetch tool; Crazy Masala was verified in the browser pane instead. Magicpin blog and District blog were not opened.
- No email addresses, phone numbers or DM routes were collected beyond contact pages named above. Contact details for each target still need to be taken from their own pages.
- Absence is not proof: "not mentioned" means not found on the page as fetched, and "no Facebook/TripAdvisor listing" means none was found by search and a direct lookup.
