# EARN: independent mentions (GEO Steps 4 and 5)

Honest only. No fake, bought, incentivised or gated reviews. No fake accounts, undisclosed paid mentions, or posts pretending to be neutral customers. Follow each platform's policies.

## 1. Claim and contest (Step 4)

1. Work through every row of `docs/geo/presence-audit.csv`. For each source: claim it, fix name, address, phone, hours (only when verified), categories, menu, descriptor and photos, and write the change in the `issues` column.
2. Wrong data in a directory or an AI answer: find the source the answer came from, correct it there first, then send feedback through the engine's own option. Log each in `docs/geo/corrections-log.csv`.
3. Duplicates and ghost listings: report and merge duplicate Google Business Profile or directory entries; record the listing IDs and ticket numbers.
4. Dispute templates for EazyDiner, Justdial and parking are in `docs/brihadhee-aeo-ops.md` section 3. Send them only after the owner confirms that Brihadhee is 100% vegetarian.

## 2. Reviews (compliant)

**Table QR card (print on the table and the bill):**
> Enjoyed your meal at Brihadhee? An honest Google review helps other people find us. Scan to leave one, whatever your experience was.

Rules:
- Ask **every** guest, not only the happy ones. Do not filter or "pre-screen" who is shown the link.
- No incentives of any kind (no discounts, free items, entries into a draw).
- No scripted or suggested wording. A neutral line such as "tell others about your visit" is fine; do not tell people what to say.
- Staff must not review on behalf of guests or from the restaurant's Wi-Fi.

**WhatsApp follow-up** (only to people who have given their number for a reason such as a call or enquiry):
> Hi [Name], thank you for visiting Brihadhee. If you have a minute, you can leave an honest Google review here: [link]. Thank you.

**Reply templates** (reply within 2 to 3 days; always use the reviewer's name and mention one specific thing they wrote):

- Positive:
  > Thank you, [Name]. We are glad you enjoyed the [dish they mentioned]. You are always welcome at Brihadhee, Anna Nagar East. Team Brihadhee
- Mixed:
  > Thank you for your honest feedback, [Name]. We are glad you liked [positive point]. About [issue], we are sorry it fell short, and we are looking into it. If you would like to talk, please call +91 73587 14113. Team Brihadhee
- Negative:
  > [Name], we are sorry about your experience with [issue]. This is not how we want a visit to Brihadhee to go. Please call or WhatsApp us on +91 73587 14113 so we can understand what happened. Team Brihadhee

Never argue, never reveal customer details, never offer something in exchange for changing a review. Do not claim anything unconfirmed in a reply (hours, booking, parking, offers).

## 3. Roundup articles and "best of" lists

Targets are collected in `docs/geo/outreach-targets.md` (researched, with the evidence for each). Process per outlet:
1. Read three of their recent restaurant pieces and note how they pick places.
2. Pitch only something real: a dish, the banana-leaf meal service, a new menu item. The Build Your Own Dosa idea is **not** pitched until it is live and the owner has confirmed how it works.
3. Short pitch (adapt, do not mass-send):
   > Hello [Name], I help with Brihadhee, a vegetarian South Indian restaurant at 3rd Avenue, W Block, Anna Nagar East (near Anna Tower). I enjoyed your piece on [specific article]. If you are updating your list of [Anna Nagar / vegetarian South Indian] places, we would be glad to host you for a tasting. [One true, specific detail: for example the Executive TN Meals on banana leaf.] Photos and menu: [link]. Phone/WhatsApp: +91 73587 14113.
4. Record each outreach in a simple sheet: date, outlet, contact, pitch, response, link when published. Ask for corrections only for factual errors.

## 4. Creators and bloggers
Shortlist 10 Chennai creators with an Anna Nagar or vegetarian focus (see `outreach-targets.md`).

- Offer an honest tasting. Do not script opinions, scores, wording or "must include" claims.
- **Disclosure is mandatory.** Under India's ASCI influencer guidelines, a paid or free-meal collaboration must be disclosed clearly and upfront in the post itself (not buried in hashtags or comments). Before the first collaboration, read the current ASCI guidance and put the required label for gifted or paid content in the brief. Brihadhee will check each post has it and ask the creator to add it if not.
- Brief (one page): who Brihadhee is (use the 25-word descriptor), the address, what they will be served, the disclosure requirement, that the opinion is entirely theirs, and a factual-accuracy check offered on hours, address, dish names only.
- Budget and free-meal value: **pending the client**.

## 5. Community
Where locals ask for recommendations: Reddit r/chennai (and r/ChennaiFood if present), local Facebook groups, residents' associations in Anna Nagar.
- Read the group rules first; ask a moderator when self-promotion rules are unclear.
- Reply only to threads where Brihadhee genuinely fits (vegetarian, South Indian, Anna Nagar), once per thread, and say you are connected to the restaurant:
  > I work with Brihadhee, a vegetarian South Indian restaurant at 3rd Avenue, W Block, Anna Nagar East. It serves dosas, parotta, Tamil Nadu meals and filter coffee. Menu: [link].
- Never post as a neutral customer or ask others to upvote.
- Log each thread: date, platform, URL, replied, outcome.
