### 1.3 The 10 weaknesses Brihadhee can exploit immediately

1. Our own name is not owned: "Brihadhee Anna Nagar" returns nothing about us. Fix: website on GBP, Search Console, sameAs, brand pages (Week 1).
2. EazyDiner says Brihadhee serves non-veg. Fix the listing; competitors also suffer this, so being visibly "100% pure vegetarian" everywhere is a differentiator.
3. Sangeetha's menu is an image; Gowri Krishnaa's site is dead. A crawlable priced menu answers "price" searches no one else answers.
4. Nobody near us owns "Nellai meals" or "sodhi" online. We do (₹240 Nellai Special Sodhi Meals, ₹180 Idiyappam Sodhi).
5. Nobody explains dosa varieties well locally. Our 11 dosas + customised dosa make the best "dosa varieties" page in Anna Nagar.
6. Geetham's dine-in ratings are weak. Our breakfast-from-6:30 AM, dine-in story targets that gap.
7. Karthik Tiffin Centre has no seating. We have family tables (and pre-booking offers).
8. Chains are "safe but generic". Founder-led regional Tamil food is a story they cannot copy.
9. 68 Google reviews already mention *pottalam parotta*, but our website does not mention it once. Confirm the dish and build a page; demand is proven.
10. No local rival markets live-counter catering for home functions. We are confirmed for 50 to 1,500 guests.

## 3. 90-day content battle plan

### Shared defaults (so tables stay readable)
- **Schema:** website pages get `Restaurant` + `WebSite` + `WebPage` + `BreadcrumbList` automatically. Extra types are listed per row. FAQ blocks get `FAQPage` matching the visible text exactly. Social posts carry no schema; videos embedded on the site get `VideoObject`.
- **CTA codes:** CALL = tap to call +91 94442 24999; DIR = Google Maps directions; BOOK = pre-book on Swiggy Dineout / EazyDiner / District; MENU = /menu/; CATER = catering enquiry.
- **Links codes:** H = home; M = /menu/; L = /about/ (location and hours; later /location/anna-nagar/); D = dosa hub; N = Nellai page; C = catering; B = book a table.
- **Metric codes:** RANK = position for the target keyword (organic and local pack); CTR = Search Console click-through rate; ACT = GBP actions (calls + directions + website clicks); AI = cited in AI answers for the matching prompt (monthly check).
- **Refresh:** M = monthly, Q = quarterly, S = once a season, E = every time the menu or offer changes.
- **Gated** rows wait for the owner fact in brackets.

### 6.8 Main risks and how to reduce them

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Publishing a fact that is wrong (hours, parking, 500+) | Site build fails if an unconfirmed fact renders (`check:facts`). Team checks the owner-todo before any post |

| Review policy breach (incentives, gating) | Ask everyone, offer nothing, never ask for 5 stars |

| Creator posts with wrong facts | Give every creator a one-page fact sheet; ask to correct errors |
| Swiggy/Zomato listings keep showing wrong data | Monthly correction check; keep screenshots |
| Team bandwidth | One owner per channel; weekly 30-minute review |
| Over-reliance on aggregators | Every channel links back to the website and GBP |
| Name confusion with Brihadeeswarar Temple | Consistent full phrase "Brihadhee restaurant, Anna Nagar"; temple disclaimer in llms.txt and About FAQ |
